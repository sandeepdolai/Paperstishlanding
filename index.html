GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

What is Paper Stish?+

Paper Stish is a creative space for digital gifts, personalized website templates, photo editing, and photo stories. Make a page around a person or a moment, then publish and share it.

Do I need design experience?+

No. Templates give you somewhere to begin. The personal part is in the words, photos, and details you choose to add.

Can I use my own photos and words?+

Yes. The idea is to bring your own words and photos into a design, so the final page feels personal rather than pre-written.

How do I share what I make?+

After publishing a page, you can share it with a link or a QR code, making it easy for someone to open on their device.
