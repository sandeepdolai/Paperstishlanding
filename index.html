GOOD TO KNOW

you might wonder.

What is Paper Stish?+

Paper Stish is a creative space for digital gifts, personalized website templates, photo editing, and photo stories. Make a page around a person or a moment, then publish and share it.

Are the template previews real?+

Yes. The artwork shown on this page comes from the Paper Stish website-template library. The template links open the product workspace.

Can I use my own photos and words?+

Yes. The goal is to bring your own words and photos into a template so the finished page feels personal.

How do I share what I make?+

When your page is ready to publish, you can share it with a link or QR code. Publishing features depend on your account and plan.
